How a campaign day works.

The same offline phone app as ReMA, set up for activation sites instead of store routes.

  1. Check in at the site

    GPS and a fresh selfie, the same check-in as ReMA, so the team leader knows who is on the stand and when.

  2. Log each engagement

    One tap per consumer spoken to, timestamped, for each brand ambassador.

  3. Count the crowd

    Photograph the activation area. AI returns a headcount in seconds, stamped with site, time and ambassador.

  4. Confirmed by the consumer

    A QR code on the stand opens WhatsApp with a code. The consumer ticks consent and gets the offer. Each phone counts once a day.

  5. In their own words

    With the consumer's consent, a voice note of up to 30 seconds on what they thought.

  6. The evening report at 8pm

    The whole day by site and by ambassador, e-mailed to the brand and the agency.
