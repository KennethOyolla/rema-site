Common questions

Do merchandisers need company e-mail?

No. Any personal e-mail works for sign-in, such as Gmail. Supervisors often use their work address.

What phones does it need?

Any recent smartphone with a camera, GPS and a browser such as Chrome. Nothing to install from an app store.

Who owns the data?

You do. Each company's data is kept separate and is never shown to another client. We'll sign an NDA before you share your lists.

How long are photos kept?

Six months by default. Dashboards and reports keep their numbers after photos are cleared.

Can we run it outside Kenya?

Yes. ReMA is already in use in Uganda, and currency and time zone are set per company.
