Field teams work hard. Most of it is invisible by Monday.

Reports arrive late, or not at all

Photos sit in WhatsApp groups and Excel sheets get filled on Friday. By the time an out-of-stock reaches a manager, the sale is gone.

Shelf share is a guess

Share of shelf, prices off RRP and competitor activity are eyeballed, not measured, so the conversation with the principal is opinion against opinion.

Pay rests on trust

Days worked, store hours and visits per merchandiser are hard to prove, which makes paying a field team of 30 slow and disputed.

One visit, start to finish, on any phone.

Built for merchandisers on budget Android phones with patchy signal. Everything works offline and uploads when the network returns.

  1. Check in at the store

    GPS confirms they're inside the store's geofence, with a fresh selfie. Checked again on the server.

  2. Availability and price

    Tick the store's portfolio in stock or out, and enter shelf prices. Anything off RRP is flagged.

  3. Photograph the shelf

    AI counts facings by brand and returns your share of shelf against target in seconds.

  4. Competitor activity and documents

    Stamped photos of promotions and displays; scans of GRNs and purchase orders.

  5. Check out

    Time in store is recorded, and the visit lands on the manager's dashboard.

20:00Sunday

Monday's meeting, ready before you sleep.

Every Sunday at 8pm ReMA e-mails a ten-slide team review as PowerPoint and PDF. Open it Monday morning and present. No one stays late building it.

Each slide compares this week with last week and month to date, so you see what moved, not just where you are. The AI drafts the insights and key actions; you edit them and assign owners.

  • AttendanceVisits, days worked, average start and end of day per merchandiser
  • All outletsAvailability, price compliance and share of shelf, week on week
  • By chainUp to four chains, each with its weakest stores named
  • DocumentsGRNs, purchase orders and returns scanned this week
  • Insights and key actionsDrafted by AI from the week's numbers, with owners
  • CelebrationThe stores and people who did best, for the team chat
