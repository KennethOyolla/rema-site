Field sales and pre-sellers

Your reps already visit the store. ReMA lets them check the shelf and take the order in the same visit, so every call ends with stock on the way, not a promise.

  • Orders from the store's catalogue in pieces or cases, with the right discount for that chain or outlet
  • Stamped LPOs, cheque photos and mobile-money proof attached to the order
  • Orders e-mailed to your order desk at once, plus an end-of-day spreadsheet
  • Calls, productive calls, lines per call and value by chain, rep and product
  • Works in general trade and modern trade, offline when there's no signal

Why one app for both

The rep who sees the empty facing should be the one who fills it. When the audit and the order live in the same visit, you can see whether out-of-stocks turn into orders, store by store.
