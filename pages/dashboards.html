The weekly team review

Ten slides, as PowerPoint and PDF, in your inbox every Sunday at 8pm Nairobi time. Present it on Monday, or forward it to your principal as it is.

Every number shows this week, last week and month to date. Insights and key actions are drafted by AI from the week's data for you to edit. Modules you don't use are left out of the deck automatically.

  • AttendanceVisits, days worked, hours, short visits, average start and end of day
  • All outletsAvailability, price compliance and share of shelf with week-on-week change
  • ChainsUp to four chain slides, each naming its weakest stores
  • DocumentsWhat was scanned this week and month to date
  • Insights and key actionsOne insight and one action per chain, with an owner
  • CelebrationTop stores and top people of the week

The monthly team report, for HR and payroll

At 7am on the 1st of each month ReMA e-mails last month's merchandiser report to the people your admin chooses: the facts you need to pay a field team fairly, without arguments.

  • Visits and days worked
  • Average start and end of the working day
  • Average availability, price compliance and share of shelf in their stores
  • Recipients set by your company admin
